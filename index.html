01 / OUR MISSION

A little warmth
goes a long way.

The Sock Bike was founded on a simple belief: meaningful change doesn't always require grand gestures.

In New York City, where harsh winters can make life especially difficult for those experiencing homelessness, something as simple as a clean, warm pair of socks can provide much-needed comfort.

Through direct, outreach, our mission is to provide practical support to members of our community who need it most.

We believe in making a tangible difference, using our resources thoughtfully, and treating every person with dignity and compassion.
